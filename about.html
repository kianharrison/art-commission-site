Kian Harrison

HEY, I'M KIAN.

Kian Harrison, known online as KianLooksBetter, is a digital artist, animator and storyteller focused on character-driven visuals and meaningful motion. He blends expressive illustration with polished storytelling to make each piece feel intentional and memorable.

His work spans portraits, character design, short animation and story-led visual communication. The creative direction is shaped by empathy, clarity and personality, whether he is working on a commission, a personal project, or a short narrative piece.

He approaches each project with careful planning, honest feedback and a strong eye for detail, making sure the final result reflects both the client’s intention and the artistic voice behind the work.

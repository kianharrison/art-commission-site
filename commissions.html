Terms

PROJECT TERMS

  • Payment: All transactions are handled via PayPal. A 50% deposit is required upfront.
  • Revisions: Progress sketches are shared for approval and minor revisions are included before final delivery.
  • Timeline: Creative work may require a short grace period around very tight deadlines.
  • Cancellation: Projects are subject to the agreed terms before work begins.
  • Commercial usage: Commercial rights must be discussed and agreed separately.
  • Copyright: I reserve the right to post completed work in my portfolio where appropriate.
  • Content restrictions: I do not accept commissions involving NSFW, heavy gore, or hateful content.

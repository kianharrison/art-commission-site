About Kian

HEY, I'M KIAN.

I'm a digital artist and animator from Cameroon, creating illustrations and animated stories centred around characters, humour and visual storytelling.

I make work that blends expressive illustration with movement, whether it is a portrait, a character concept, or a short animated story with personality.

I don't just make client work. I also build personal stories, experiments, and character-driven pieces that keep the creative voice alive beyond commissions.
